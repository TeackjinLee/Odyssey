Odysseus as a cute stocky CHIBI hero (about 3 heads tall), exactly like concept.png: bronze Corinthian helmet with gold scroll ornaments and a tall red horsehair crest sweeping back, face visible (dark eyebrows, brown eyes, short dark beard), red cape over both shoulders with round gold shoulder clasps, bronze muscle cuirass with a diagonal brown leather strap and a Greek-key belt, leather pteruges with crimson panels over a cream skirt, bronze bracers, bronze greaves with knee guards, sandals.
Weapons always carried: a short bronze leaf-blade sword with a gold crossguard held in the RIGHT fist, a round bronze shield with a gold eight-pointed star strapped to the LEFT forearm, and a wooden recurve bow with a brown quiver of white-fletched arrows slung diagonally on his BACK.
